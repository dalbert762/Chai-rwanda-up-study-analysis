Findings

Findings and recommended actions

Finding Evidence Action
Solace Ministries CS answered 'Brand' on all 44 of its items 44 of the 46 brand-typed rows in the dataset; every other facility answered 'Brand' at most once. All 44 carry an INN name picked from the EML list and all 44 are flagged on-EML. One collector reading the question differently, not a brand-prescribing signal. Re-brief that collector; treat Name type as unusable for Solace until re-collected.
